## 1. Overview & Core Concept

### 1. Islamic Inheritance (*Ilm al-Mirath*) Calculator
- **Estate Deductions Wizard**: Gross estate value minus funeral expenses (*Tajheez*), debts/unpaid liabilities (including unpaid Mahr & loans), and bequest (*Wasiyyah*, capped at maximum 1/3) to arrive at the Net Distributable Estate (*Al-Tirka*).
- **Heirs Selection**: Deceased gender, surviving spouse(s), sons, daughters, father, mother, paternal grandfather, grandmothers, full brothers/sisters, paternal siblings, and maternal siblings.
- **Quranic Share Engine**: Exact fractions ($1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 2/3, 1/3, 1/6$) and Residuary (*Asabah* $2:1$), plus automatic *'Awl* and *Al-Radd*.
- **4 Madhab Selector**: Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali comparative rulings on disputed areas (Grandfather with Siblings, Spouse Radd eligibility).

### 2. Islamic Hijri Events & Sunnah Fasting Calendar
- **12 Hijri Months**: Lunar calendar grid with day-by-day mapping and $\pm 2$ day moonsighting declaration adjustments.
- **Sacred Days Countdown**: Live countdown to Ramadan, Laylat al-Qadr, Eid al-Fitr, Day of Arafah, Eid al-Adha, Ashura (10th Muharram), Islamic New Year, Isra & Mi'raj, and Mid-Sha'ban.
- **Sunnah Fasting Tracker**: White Days (*Ayyām al-Bīḍ* - 13th, 14th, 15th), Mondays & Thursdays, 6 Days of Shawwal, and 9 Days of Dhul Hijjah with 1-tap logging and streak tracker.

### 3. Halal Food & E-Numbers Guide
- **350+ Additives Database**: Comprehensive offline catalog of E-numbers (E100 to E1521).
- **Three-Tier Status**: **Halal** (Permissible - Green), **Haram** (Prohibited - Red), **Mushbooh** (Doubtful / Source-dependent - Amber).
- **Source Breakdown**: Animal, Vegetable, Synthetic, Microbial, Mineral with search by code or ingredient name.

### 4. Makkah & Madinah 24/7 Live Haramain Streams
- **Live Streams**: Masjid al-Haram (Makkah Quran TV) and Masjid an-Nabawi (Madinah Sunnah TV).
- **Dual Digital Clock**: Local Time vs. Makkah Standard Time with real-time prayer schedule indicators.

### 5. Khatam Quran Target Planner
- **Target Pacing Wizard**: Choose completion target (30 Days / Ramadan, 60 Days, 1 Year, or Custom Days) and target Juz/Pages per prayer.
- **Per-Prayer Check-in**: Pacing breakdowns (e.g. 4 pages after Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) with 1-tap completion logs.
- **Visual Progress Ring & Khatam Du'a**: Track total completed Juz and access the authentic prophetic *Du'a Khatm al-Quran*.

### 6. Audio-Enabled Quranic Ruqyah & Spiritual Healing
- **Ayat ash-Shifa & Protection Verses**: Al-Fatihah, Ayat al-Kursi, Al-Baqarah 285-286, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas, and authentic Sunnah supplications for sickness, anxiety, grief, and the evil eye (*Ayn*).
- **Audio Engine**: Text-to-Speech audio playback for every healing passage.
- **Prophetic Method Walkthrough**: Step-by-step Sunnah guide to performing Ruqyah (niyyah, cupping hands, recitation, blowing with light spit *Nafath*, wiping the afflicted area).

### 7. Islamic Baby Names & Meanings Dictionary
- **Authentic Database**: 200+ curated Islamic names for boys and girls with Arabic script, transliteration, English meaning, and Quranic/Prophetic history.
- **Filters & Search**: Filter by Boys, Girls, Unisex, and initial letter with favorite bookmarking.
- **Sunnah of Naming**: Prophetic advice on choosing good names, *Tahneek*, and *Aqiqah*.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

| Decision | Chosen Approach | Rationale | Alternatives Considered |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inheritance Mathematical Precision** | Kotlin exact rational fractions ($N/D$) scaled into decimals and currency amounts | Prevents floating-point rounding errors when distributing estates with shares like $1/8, 1/6, 2/3$ | Primitive `Double` division (causes rounding cent loss) |
| **Comparative Madhab Jurisprudence** | Full 4 Madhab selector (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali) with explicit fiqh citations | Honors classical consensus and allows users to calculate according to their specific legal school | Single-school simplified model (disregards valid Madhab differences) |
| **Khatam Quran Pacing Strategy** | Dynamic prayer-based distribution (e.g. 4 pages after each of the 5 daily prayers) | Makes reading 1 Juz per day realistic and achievable through micro-habits rather than intimidating 20-page blocks | Daily-only lump sum target |
| **Offline-First Data Architecture** | Embedded Kotlin data structures for all 7 modules (E-codes, Baby Names, Ruqyah, Hijri Calendar, Mirath) | 100% functional offline with zero network latency and no external API keys required | Remote cloud REST endpoints (fails without active internet) |
